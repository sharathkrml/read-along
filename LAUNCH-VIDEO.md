# Read Along — launch video script

Your draft, expanded. Same order, same voice, same beats. Every claim below
exists in the current build.

**Not in this script** (killed in the app): Read to me / text-to-speech, auto
page turn, split view, Present, Edit text, library. Only Follow me.

---

## The script

### 0:00–0:04 · Your opening
**VO:** I love making videos like this, and I love reading stuff online.
**VISUAL:** You at the desk, a document open. Relaxed, not a pitch.

### 0:04–0:08 · The problem
**VO:** But the sad part? I zone out. I'll read a line and literally forget what
I just read.
**VISUAL:** A page of text. Eyes drift off it.

### 0:08–0:13 · The thing everyone recognises
**VO:** So then I'm sitting there, moving my cursor around, trying to figure out
which line I was actually on.
**VISUAL:** The cursor hunt. Sweep it up and down the paragraph, looking for the
place. Hold this shot — it's the hook. Nobody needs it explained.

### 0:13–0:15 · The turn
**VO:** Which is exactly why I built this app.
**VISUAL:** Hard cut to the app. No transition.

### 0:15–0:19 · What you do
**VO:** You upload a PDF, or just a photo of a page from a book.
**VISUAL:** Drag a photo of a book page into the window. The page appears.

### 0:19–0:24 · What it does
**VO:** And as you read, it keeps track of where you are. Word by word.
**VISUAL:** Press Follow me. Read aloud. The amber box moves with your voice.

### 0:24–0:29 · The two boxes
**VO:** The amber box is the words you're on. The blue box is the words right
after it.
**VISUAL:** Zoom in on the page. Freeze for a second and label both boxes.
**TEXT:** amber = now · blue = next

### 0:29–0:35 · The models
**VO:** The handwriting OCR is LFM3-VL. The speech is Phonon-2.5.
**VISUAL:** Cut to the real Settings model card so the names are on screen, not
just in your mouth.

### 0:35–0:38 · Why it matters
**VO:** Both run on this Mac. No account, no cloud.
**VISUAL:** Wi-Fi off in the menu bar. Cut back, still following.

### 0:38–0:42 · CTA
**VO:** If you want to try it, the link is in the description.
**VISUAL:** App icon. Name. Link card.

## Your line → the line to say

| Your draft | Expanded |
|---|---|
| "I son out" | I zone out |
| "I literally forget the line I just read" | I'll read a line and literally forget what I just read |
| "trying putting my cursor trying to figure out which line" | moving my cursor around, trying to figure out which line I was actually on |
| "keeps the track of you, track of whatever you're reading" | keeps track of where you are, word by word |
| "shows the line that you are reading and the line after" | amber box is the words you're on, blue box is the words right after |
| "LFM for object character recognition or OCR" | LFM3-VL, the handwriting OCR model |
| "phonon for text-to-speech. Sorry, speech to text." | Phonon-2.5, the speech model — say it once, no apology |
| "link in description or comments" | link is in the description |

Two wording notes:

- **"line" → "words."** The box covers a phrase, not a whole line. On camera the
  difference is obvious, so don't say line.
- **"upload" is fine** as long as the on-device line lands right after it.
  Otherwise people assume there's a server.

---

## Production

- One continuous recording of the cursor moving is worth more than any cut. That
  shot is your hook and your proof.
- Record at 2x, then crop tight to the page when you go vertical. Full-window
  recordings are unreadable on a phone.
- Burn in captions. The two model names need to be readable text, not audio.
